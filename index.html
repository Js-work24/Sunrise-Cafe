Our Story

A Table Worth Coming Back To

Sunrise Café was born from a simple idea — that breakfast and lunch deserve the same care as any fine dinner. We source from local farms, bake our bread in-house each morning, and brew with single-origin beans roasted just down the road.

Since opening in 2019, we've become the neighborhood's favorite place to slow down, reconnect, and eat something truly worth waking up for.
